Your government, closer to you

A citizen shouldn't need to know which department is responsible before trying to solve a problem. LOKSETU's Departments directory lets you search by what you need — a certificate, a subsidy, a complaint — and shows the right department, its services, schemes and official contacts.

Where does our data come from?

LOKSETU prioritises information from the Government of Maharashtra, the Maharashtra State Data Bank, the Directorate of Economics and Statistics, and official department and district portals. Every statistic on this page is shown with its reference year, and every scheme or department profile links back to its official source where one is available. LOKSETU is an independent citizen project and is not an official Government of Maharashtra website unless stated — see our disclaimer.

Information. Access. Connection.

From a major city to the smallest village, every part of Maharashtra is connected through its people, institutions, administration, services and communities. LOKSETU brings these connections together in one place.
