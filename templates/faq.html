Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about the LokSetu platform.

1

What is LokSetu?

LokSetu is a comprehensive E‑Governance and Public Service Management Platform designed to enhance transparency, accountability, and citizen engagement in government administration. The platform provides a centralized digital ecosystem where citizens can register Complaints, access government schemes, track public development projects, monitor fund utilization, and interact with government authorities through a secure and transparent workflow.
2

How can I submit a Complaint?

To submit a Complaint, navigate to the Submit Complaint page on the website. Fill in the required information including your personal details, address, Complaint title and description, select the appropriate department, and submit the form. You can also attach supporting documents and mark the exact location on the map.
3

How can I track my Complaint?

Once your Complaint is submitted, you will receive a unique Complaint ID. Use this ID on the Track Complaint page to check the current progress and status of your Complaint. You will also receive email or SMS updates (if provided) as your Complaint moves through the resolution workflow.
4

Which departments are available on LokSetu?

LokSetu supports Complaints related to several government departments, including:
  • Water Supply Department
  • Electricity Department
  • Roads and Infrastructure
  • Sanitation and Waste Management
  • Health Services
  • Education Services
5

How long does it take to resolve a Complaint?

The resolution time varies depending on the type of issue and the department responsible. LokSetu helps speed up the process by sending Complaints directly to the concerned authorities. Most Complaints are acknowledged within 48 hours and resolved within 7–10 working days, subject to the complexity of the issue and departmental workload.
6

Who can use LokSetu?

LokSetu is designed for all citizens residing in Maharashtra. The platform is accessible to anyone — from urban residents to rural villagers — who wishes to report public service issues, access government schemes, or track development projects. Users can register with basic details and start using the platform immediately.
7

Is LokSetu free to use?

Yes, LokSetu is completely free for all citizens. The platform is a government initiative designed to make public services more accessible and transparent. There are no charges for submitting Complaints, tracking progress, or accessing scheme information.

Help & Support

LokSetu is designed to help citizens report public service issues quickly and easily. Through this platform, users can submit Complaints related to village infrastructure and track their status online.

If your question is not answered in the FAQ section, please reach out to the LokSetu support team for assistance.

Quick Tip

After submitting a Complaint, you will receive a unique Complaint ID. Keep this ID safe as it will help you track the progress and updates of your Complaint.

Pro Tip: Upload clear photos or documents with your Complaint to help authorities understand and resolve the issue faster.
