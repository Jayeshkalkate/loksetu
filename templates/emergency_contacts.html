Emergency Contact Numbers

Immediate assistance numbers for citizens across Maharashtra and India.

National Emergency Helpline

  • 112 National Emergency Helpline All emergencies (Police, Fire, Ambulance)
  • 100 Police Law enforcement and public safety
  • 101 Fire Brigade Fire emergencies and rescue
  • 102 Ambulance Emergency medical transport
  • 108 Emergency Medical Service 24×7 medical assistance

Women & Child Safety

  • 1091 Women Helpline Support for women in distress
  • 1098 Child Helpline Protection and support for children

Disaster Management

  • 1070 State Disaster Management Helpline Natural disaster coordination
  • 1077 District Disaster Control Room Local disaster response

Health & Medical Services

  • 104 Health Helpline Medical advice and health information
  • 108 Emergency Medical Transport Ambulance and emergency care

Important: For non-emergency public service complaints such as water supply, electricity, sanitation, or roads, please use the LokSetu complaint submission system.

Emergency Help

In case of emergencies, citizens can contact the following helpline numbers for immediate assistance. These services are available across Maharashtra and India.

Important Advice

For urgent situations such as accidents, fire hazards, medical emergencies, or law enforcement support, please use the official emergency numbers listed here.

Emergency TIPS:

  • Describe the situation briefly.
